What to know before you take a closer look.

What is Coalign?

Coalign is a shared context platform for engineering teams building with coding agents. Our first focus is integration review: connect a proposed change to the intent behind both branches, its supporting evidence, and clear human decisions.

Can I try it today?

You can explore the interactive review example on this page. Coalign itself is in development; live repository integration, deployment, and customer acceptance are still in progress.

Does it replace my coding agent?

The product direction is to carry useful context from supported coding-agent work into your team’s review process. The first scope is one agent integration, GitHub, and a selected repository and language environment.

Who decides whether a patch ships?

Your team. Review acceptance, commit, and merge are separate human decisions. Changed revisions require a fresh review; insufficient evidence or conflicting requirements can produce a question or an abstention.
